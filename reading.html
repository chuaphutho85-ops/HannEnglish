African continent splitting into two parts

Source: Breaking News English • Level 2

Scientists say there are signs that a large part of East Africa may slowly separate from the African continent in the future. Researchers studied a very deep crack in Zambia called a "rift". They believe this rift shows movement under the Earth's surface.

A geologist from Oxford University explained that the rift is active. This could be an early sign that sub-Saharan Africa may eventually split into two parts over millions of years.

Long ago, Earth had only one giant supercontinent called Pangea. Over time, it broke apart and formed the continents we know today. Scientists believe the same geological process is still happening now.

The crack in Zambia is part of a 2,500-kilometre rift system. Although it moves very slowly — about 4.7 millimetres each year — scientists think this movement could eventually create a new ocean.

Vết nứt lớn trên bề mặt Trái Đất.
