Resilient

Meaning: able to recover quickly from difficulties.

💬 Idiom of the Day

Break the ice

Meaning: start conversation in a social situation.

Example: He told a joke to break the ice.

✍ Grammar Tip

Grammar Focus

Present Perfect

Used for past actions with present relevance.

Example: I have studied English for 3 years.
